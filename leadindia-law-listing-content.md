## About Sourabh Rawat

Advocate Sourabh Rawat is a Lucknow-based lawyer enrolled with the Bar Council of Uttar Pradesh, practising for over 10 years across the District & Sessions Court, Lucknow and the High Court of Judicature at Allahabad, Lucknow Bench. He handles criminal, civil, and family law matters, with a focus on bail applications, FIR defense and quashing, property disputes, and divorce and custody cases.

His practice includes regular and anticipatory bail at the Magistrate, Sessions Court, and High Court level; FIR quashing petitions under Section 528 BNSS (earlier Section 482 CrPC); property and civil disputes including injunctions and recovery suits; and family law matters such as divorce, child custody, maintenance, and domestic violence cases at Family Court, Lucknow. He also provides on-ground legal assistance at police stations across Lucknow — including Madiyaon, Kaiserbagh, Hazratganj, Gomti Nagar, Aliganj, and Indira Nagar — for arrest, detention, and FIR-related matters.

Advocate Rawat runs two chambers in Lucknow — Madiyaon (616/188/A, Near Primary School, Thana Madiyaon) and Kaiserbagh (Near CHC Building, Gate No. 8) — making him accessible to clients across north, central, and south Lucknow. He offers direct client access with a response time within 2 hours, and consultations can be booked by phone or WhatsApp at +91 90263 49246.
